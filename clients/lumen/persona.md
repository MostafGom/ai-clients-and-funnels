# Persona — lumen (Lumen Sleep)

**Who**: First-time (sometimes second-time) parents of a baby or toddler aged 4 months to 3 years. Mostly the mother, 28–40, often back at work or about to be, partner supportive but "sleeps through everything". Household income $70k–$180k. Lives on Instagram and parenting Facebook groups at 3 a.m.
**Awareness**: Painfully problem-aware. Solution-aware in a messy way: has read 40 contradictory blog posts, knows the words "sleep training", "wake windows", "Ferber", and is afraid of most of them.
**Pains**:
- "I haven't slept more than 3 hours in a row in 8 months."
- Bedtime takes 90 minutes of rocking, bouncing, and a 4th rendition of the same song.
- Night wakings every 1–2 hours; co-sleeping by accident; resentment creeping into the marriage.
- Guilt either way: "I don't want to let her cry" vs "I can't keep doing this."
- Advice overload: grandma, pediatrician, the internet all disagree.
**Desires**:
- A baby who goes down in 10 minutes and sleeps 11 hours. Naps that are predictable.
- Evenings back. A date night. Feeling like a person again.
- A plan that feels kind, works fast, and doesn't require a PhD.
**Objections**:
- "I don't want to let my baby cry it out."
- "Every baby is different, this won't work for mine." (reflux, twins, daycare, teething)
- "I've tried everything already."
- "My partner won't stick to it." / "We travel next month."
**Language they use**: tiny human, night owl, wake windows, regression, "touched out", contact naps, "the 4-month regression", "split nights", "sleep like a baby (ha)".
**Proof that moves them**: before/after night logs (screenshots of baby monitors, Huckleberry charts), video testimonials from mums who look rested, pediatric credentials, "no cry-it-out" stated explicitly, number of families helped.

## Copy angles
1. **Outcome**: "11 hours. Every night. In 7 nights."
2. **Mechanism**: "It's not about crying. It's about timing: the 3 wake windows nobody explained to you."
3. **Enemy**: "Stop googling at 3 a.m. The internet is why you're still awake."
4. **Identity**: "You're not failing. You were handed a tiny human and zero instructions."
5. **Speed**: "Tonight is night one."
