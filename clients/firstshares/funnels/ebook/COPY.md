4. **Is this advice for my situation?** No. It's education, the same for everyone. For personal advice, talk to a fee-only fiduciary adviser.
5. **What happens after I enter my email?** The PDF lands in about a minute. Then five short emails over a week, one idea each. Unsubscribe in one click.
