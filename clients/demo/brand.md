# Brand brief — demo (Northwind Coaching)

**Personality**: calm, precise, grounded; a senior operator, not a guru. Confident but never hyped.
**Voice**: short sentences, concrete numbers, plain verbs. Says “plan”, “rhythm”, “tracker”. Never “crush”, “10x”, “secret”.
**Visual direction**: editorial warmth — serif display (Fraunces) over a neutral sans (Inter); off-white paper background; deep blue primary; honey accent for one-time offers only; generous whitespace; large rounded cards; soft layered shadows.
**Do**: one accent moment per screen; real photography or typographic cards instead of stock illustrations.
**Don't**: gradients on text, red urgency, multiple CTAs per screen, emoji in headlines.
**Tokens**: `web/src/styles/clients/demo.css` · verified with `node design/contrast.mjs`.
