# Persona — demo (Northwind Coaching)

**Who**: Independent consultants and fractional leaders (ops, finance, brand, UX, HR) billing $5k–$40k/month, 2–8 years solo. 35–50 years old. Good at the work, uneven at selling it.
**Awareness**: Problem-aware (feast-or-famine), skeptical of “marketing systems”, allergic to cold outreach and ads.
**Pains**: pipeline lives three months behind delivery; referrals feel random; Mondays without a plan; shame of “hustling”.
**Desires**: predictable months, 3–5 qualified calls a week, a calm weekly rhythm, no persona change.
**Objections**: “My niche is different”, “I don't have time”, “I hate selling”, “I've bought courses before”.
**Language they use**: pipeline, retainers, discovery call, scope, fractional, referral, dry spell.
**Proof that moves them**: peers with numbers (calls/week, MRR), specificity (scripts, trackers), low time cost.
