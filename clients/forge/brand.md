# Brand brief — forge (Forge Fitness Online)

**Who**: Forge Fitness Online, founded by Daniel Reyes, strength coach (15 years; former physiotherapist, 1,900+ clients since 2011). Method: **The Forge Protocol**: three 45-minute sessions a week, six fundamental lifts, autoregulated progression, 12-week cycles. Built for men 35–50.

**Personality**:
- *Calm*: unhurried sentences, no exclamation marks, no countdowns. The brand never raises its voice.
- *Authoritative*: specific numbers, named lifts, named mechanisms. Says "here's what we do" rather than "imagine if."
- *Respectful*: speaks to the reader as a capable adult with a full life. Never shames, never "excuses."

**Voice**: measured, precise, plain. Second person. Short paragraphs. Verbs: build, progress, train, recover. Use: session, lift, progression, recovery, "train around," 12 weeks. Ban: "crush," "beast," "grind," "shred," "no excuses," "hack," transformation-photo language, anything shouted.

**Visual direction**: luxurious and quiet. Onyx surfaces dominant (hero, proof, close) with warm bone paper for reading. Brass accent used sparingly (rules, numerals, one-time offers); deep copper for the single action per screen. Elegant serif display (Cormorant Garamond) at large sizes, clean grotesk body (Manrope). Hairline rules instead of cloud edges. Photography: real men 35–50 in calm gyms and home racks, low light, no shirtless shots. Art: thin-line marks (plates, a bar, a 12-week ledger), numerals as design.

**Do**: generous whitespace, one brass moment per screen, tables and ledgers for proof, large serif numerals.
**Don't**: red, neon, lightning bolts, stock "ripped" imagery, countdowns, all-caps, emoji.

**Tokens**: `web/src/styles/clients/forge.css` · verified with `node design/contrast.mjs`.
