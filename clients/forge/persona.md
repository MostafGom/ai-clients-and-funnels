# Persona — forge (Forge Fitness Online)

**Who**: Men 35–50 who run things: founders, directors, senior engineers, surgeons, lawyers. Household income $150k+. Married, often with young kids. Used to be fit (played sport, lifted in their twenties); now 10–20 kg heavier, stiff, "no time." Trains sporadically, mostly alone, mostly guessing. Reads on his phone between meetings and late at night.
**Awareness**: Problem-aware and mildly ashamed. Solution-aware in the sense that he knows "lifting weights" is the answer and suspects the six-day bro programs are not for him. Skeptical of hype, transformation photos and anyone who shouts.
**Pains**:
- "I don't have 90 minutes a day. I have 45, three times a week, if nothing blows up."
- Back twinges, a shoulder that clicks, a knee that's "fine" until it isn't.
- Energy crash at 3 p.m.; sleep is poor; belt notches going the wrong way.
- Has started and quit four apps. Every program assumes a 24-year-old's recovery.
- Doesn't want to be the oldest guy in a commercial gym figuring out the machines.
**Desires**:
- Visibly stronger and leaner in 12 weeks, without it becoming a second job.
- A plan he can trust and stop thinking about. Told what to do, in order.
- Fewer aches; the energy to carry kids and work a long day.
- Dignity: a coach who speaks to him like an adult.
**Objections**:
- "I've got an old back / shoulder injury."
- "I only have three days and a home rack, or a hotel gym."
- "I don't want to eat chicken and rice six times a day."
- "I've bought programs before. I stop at week three."
- "Is this for men my age or a repackaged bodybuilding plan?"
**Language he uses**: busy, consistent, sustainable, "get back to," strong, lean, "train around," mobility, "I'm not 25," hotel gym, home rack, protein.
**Proof that moves him**: coaches' credentials and age; clients who look like him (45, a job, kids) with before/after numbers (lifts, waist, resting heart rate) rather than shirtless photos; specificity about injuries and time; a calm, non-salesy tone.

## Copy angles
1. **Outcome**: "Stronger at 45 than you were at 30, on three sessions a week."
2. **Mechanism**: "Three sessions, six lifts, twelve weeks. Progression does the rest."
3. **Enemy**: "You don't need more motivation. You need a program that respects your calendar and your joints."
4. **Identity**: "Built for men who run things."
5. **Speed**: "Your first session is 45 minutes. It's tonight."
