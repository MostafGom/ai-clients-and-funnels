### What happens in twenty minutes (Bone)
1. **Where you are · 5 min.** Training history, the last two weeks, injuries, equipment, the hours you actually have. We start from where you are, not where you were.
2. **Your plan · 10 min.** Daniel sets your three sessions, the lifts, the swaps for anything that hurts, and the starting weights. Written down while you talk.
3. **The next step · 5 min.** What to do on Monday, and one honest sentence about whether Forge 12 on your own, Forge 12 with coaching, or neither, fits.

### FAQ (four)
1. **Is this a sales call?** It's a plan call. If coaching fits your case, Daniel will say so in one sentence and tell you what it costs. If the free guide or the $197 program is enough, he'll say that instead. Most audits end with "start Monday at these weights."
2. **I have an injury.** That's the main reason to book. Bring what the physio or doctor said. If a doctor has told you not to lift, follow the doctor; the audit will work around it or tell you to wait.
3. **I've never lifted.** Then the audit sets your starting weights so you don't guess, and tells you which three lifts to learn first.
4. **What if work moves?** Phone or video, 6 a.m. and lunchtime slots, and you can reschedule from the invite in one tap.
