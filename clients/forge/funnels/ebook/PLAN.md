# Funnel plan — forge / ebook (free guide opt-in → thanks → nurture)

## Traffic & stage
- Source: **cold.** LinkedIn posts and ads, YouTube, podcast mentions, men sharing the planner. He's on his phone between meetings.
- Awareness: **problem-aware, mechanism-unaware.** He knows he should lift and suspects six-day programs aren't for him. The page gives the answer to a question he actually has, "how many sessions do I need?", before it asks for anything.

## Big idea
**"How many sessions a week do you actually need?"** The SessionPlanner answers in ten seconds (three, 45 minutes, here are the lifts), and the guide is the week written out.

## The lead magnet: The 3-Session Week (free, 12 pages)
The three-session template; the six lifts with form cues; the first four weeks programmed with starting-weight guidance; the eight-minute warm-up; the progression rule; the "train around" table for backs, shoulders, knees; the hotel-gym version; what to do in week five. Written in GUIDE.md, rendered at `/forge/guide/`, printed to `public/forge/assets/three-session-week.pdf`.
Why it converts: weeks one to four work, and the bar goes up. Forge 12 is weeks one to twelve with the filmed lifts, the deload, the check-ins and the bonuses.
