## 3. The real problem (Bone)
Heading: Volume isn't the lever. Progression is.
Most programs are built around how much. Six days, two hours, a split for every muscle. That works for a man with nothing else on and a body that recovers overnight. For you, it fails in week three, and the failure feels like a character flaw. It isn't. It's arithmetic.
Strength comes from three things, in this order: a small set of lifts done well; a rule that adds weight only when the last set was clean; and enough recovery that the next session is better than the last. Three sessions a week give you all three. Six give you fatigue and a reason to quit.

Heading: Twelve weeks, three phases, one rule.
Intro: Every session is the same shape: eight-minute warm-up, two main lifts, one assistance lift, done in 45 minutes. The rule: when the last set is clean, add weight next time. When it isn't, repeat. That's the entire decision.
