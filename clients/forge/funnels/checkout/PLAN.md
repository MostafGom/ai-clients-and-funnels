## Big idea
**Strength, unhurried.** Three 45-minute sessions a week, programmed properly, make a 45-year-old stronger than he was at 30. Volume isn't the lever. Progression and recovery are.

## Named mechanism
**The Forge Protocol**: six lifts (squat, bench, row, deadlift, press, pull-up), three full-body sessions, autoregulated progression (add weight when the last set is clean, repeat when it isn't), 12-week cycle with a deload in week 7.
