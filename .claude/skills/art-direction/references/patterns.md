# Section-pattern library (compose pages from these; name the pattern in DESIGN-PLAN)

## Heroes
- **Full-bleed photo hero**: photo covers the viewport, a token scrim on the side carrying type, stacked headline, CTA, and a proof strip pinned to the bottom edge. The default for physical/visual categories.
- **Split hero**: photo or visual on one half, copy on the other. On mobile the photo is cropped to ~45vh above the copy.
- **Tool hero**: the lead magnet *as an interactive tool* (planner, calculator, quiz) next to the headline. Good for opt-ins.
- **Stage hero**: a VSL on a dark stage with the headline above and a proof strip under it.

## Problem / agitation
- **Scene**: one photo plus a short, second-person narrative of the moment it hurts (e.g. 6:40 p.m., back aches).
- **Before/after table**: two columns ("how it goes now" vs "how it goes on the program"), with rows that are concrete.
- **Myth wall**: 3–4 beliefs struck through, each followed by the one-line truth.

## Mechanism
- **Number wall**: 3–4 giant numerals carrying the offer's core constraints (3 sessions · 45 min · 6 lifts · 12 weeks).
- **Timeline track**: phases laid out horizontally (scrolls sideways on mobile) with week markers.
- **Sticky-media scroll**: a pinned visual on one side while steps scroll by on the other (desktop), falling back to stacked on mobile.
- **Annotated photo**: one strong image with 3–4 callouts.

## Proof
- **Results ticker**: a marquee of specific results ("Mark, 44 — deadlift 100 → 150 kg"). Pauses on hover/focus and is static under reduced motion.
- **Ledger cards**: a person's numbers as a table, with a photo, name, age and role.
- **Proof wall**: a masonry of mixed quotes, numbers and photos (not identical cards).
- **Single big quote**: one testimonial set huge on a photo.

## Offer
- **Stack table**: line items with value, then the total, anchor and price.
- **Price block with the guarantee attached**: price and guarantee in the same visual unit.
- **Comparison**: this program vs gym membership vs personal trainer vs doing nothing.

## Trust / person
- **Coach story**: a large portrait bleeding off the edge, a short story, and credentials as numbers.
- **Who it's for / not for**: two columns, honest.

## Close
- **Photo close**: a full-bleed photo with the final headline, the CTA and the guarantee line.
- **FAQ accordion**: built from the persona's objections, never invented ones.

## Composition rules
- Alternate surface (dark/light/photo) and density. Never put two neighbours in the same pattern.
- Long pages need a photo or signature visual at least every ~2 screens.
- Put the strongest photo in the hero, not lower down.
