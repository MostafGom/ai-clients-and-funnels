# Anti-template checklist (every page; any "no" gets fixed)

1. **First viewport**: is there a photo or a subject-specific visual? (Text on a flat band alone isn't enough on start pages.)
2. **Category squint**: blur the screenshot. Does it still read as this category (gym, clinic, kitchen…)?
3. **Font fit**: does the display face carry the category's energy at 390px?
4. **Adjacent sections**: do no two neighbours share a layout pattern (centred stack → centred stack, card grid → card grid)?
5. **Rhythm**: does the page alternate density (dense proof → open statement → visual → dense detail)? Do surfaces change with intent?
6. **Signature moment**: is exactly one present, and does it work with reduced motion?
7. **Proof early**: is a specific, attributed result visible in or directly under the hero?
8. **Imagery depth**: is the strongest photo in the top third of the page, and is there real imagery at least every ~2 screens on long pages?
9. **Numbers as design**: are the offer's key numbers set large where they matter (not buried in sentences)?
10. **Kit tells**: no generic icon-in-circle triplets, no identical 3-card rows, no "01/02/03" on things that aren't sequences,
    no centred-everything, no stock gradient blobs, no "→" decoration on every button.
11. **One action**: is there one primary CTA per screen, worded identically through the flow?
12. **390px**: headline wraps on purpose, nothing overflows, tap targets ≥ 44px.
