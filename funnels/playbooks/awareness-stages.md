# Awareness stages (Schwartz) → what the page must do first

| Stage | Visitor's inner line | Page opens with | Headline shape | Lumen example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unaware | "I'm just tired, that's parenting." | A scene they recognise, not a product | Story / scenario hook | "It's 2:14 a.m. and you're doing the hallway shuffle again." |
| Problem-aware | "My baby won't sleep and I've read everything." | Name the problem more precisely than they can | "Why [problem] keeps happening (and what actually fixes it)" | "Why your baby wakes at 2 a.m. (it isn't hunger, and it isn't you)" |
| Solution-aware | "Sleep training exists but I don't want to let her cry." | The mechanism, and the enemy it replaces | "How to [result] without [the thing they fear]" | "How to get 11 hours a night without crying it out" |
| Product-aware | "I've heard of Lumen, is it worth $497?" | Proof + offer stack + guarantee immediately | Outcome + timeframe + risk reversal | "Seven nights. Sleep-through guarantee. Start tonight." |
| Most aware | "I'm in, where do I pay?" | Price, what's included, one button | Direct offer | Checkout page |

Rules
- Cold traffic (ads, social) lands on Unaware/Problem-aware pages: ebook opt-in, VSL pages.
- Warm traffic (email list, retargeting, referral) lands on Solution/Product-aware pages: sales page, calendar.
- Never ask a colder visitor to do a warmer action. An unaware visitor is not asked to buy; a most-aware visitor is not made to read 3,000 words.
- Write the stage at the top of every PLAN.md and check the first screen against this table.
