# Long-form sales page architecture (2,500–3,500 words)

Order matters. Each block has one job and hands off to the next. Skip a block only if PLAN.md says why.

1. **Hero (above the fold)** — Promise + mechanism + proof + one action.
   Headline: result + timeframe + without-the-feared-thing. Sub: names the mechanism in one sentence.
   CTA: says exactly what happens ("Start tonight · $497"). Risk line under it (guarantee, instant access).
   One characteristic visual from the subject's world. Never a stock hero.
2. **The scene** — Second-person story the reader is living right now. 120–200 words. No product.
   Ends on the question the rest of the page answers.
3. **The real problem** — Reframe: "it's not X, it's Y." Introduce the named mechanism here.
   Use one diagram/visual that teaches. The reader should feel smarter, not sold.
4. **Why what you tried didn't work** — 3–4 things they've tried, each explained kindly (why it failed in terms of the mechanism). Removes self-blame; sets up the method.
5. **Meet the founder** — Credentials + their own version of the problem + why they built this. 120–180 words. Real photo slot. Specific numbers (years, families).
6. **The method** — The named method, step by step / night by night / week by week. Show the sequence (that is where numbering belongs). Each step: what you do, what happens, how long.
7. **Proof** — Case studies, not quotes: situation → what changed → measurable result → quote. 3 of them, each a different objection profile (e.g. twins, "tried everything", daycare). Visual evidence (logs, charts, screenshots) beats adjectives. Attribution always.
8. **What you get (the stack)** — Every component with a one-line benefit and a value. Bonuses that kill a specific objection. Total value vs price. The stack is a table, not prose.
9. **Who it's for / not for** — Honest qualification. Raises trust and lowers refunds.
10. **Price** — Anchor (total value, cost of the alternative, cost of inaction), then price, then what happens after payment. Secondary option if one exists. CTA repeats the exact hero wording.
11. **Guarantee** — Named, specific, full copy: conditions, time window, how to claim, why you offer it.
12. **FAQ** — 8–12 real objections from the persona, answered directly, each ending by pointing back to the method or the guarantee.
13. **Close** — Future pacing: 7 nights from now. One paragraph. Final CTA. Sticky CTA on mobile from block 3 onwards.

Copy rules
- Headline under 14 words. Subhead under 30. Paragraphs 1–3 sentences. Line length ≤ 70 characters.
- Every section answers "so what?" for the reader, in "you" language.
- One claim per sentence. Every number is either client-verified or marked `[CLIENT]`.
- CTA text is identical everywhere on the page and matches the button on checkout.
- No exclamation marks. No ALL CAPS. No "secret", "hack", "crush". No superlatives you can't prove.
